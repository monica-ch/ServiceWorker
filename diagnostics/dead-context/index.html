Retained ServiceWorkerContainer after owner removal

Observation-only preparation for w3c/ServiceWorker#777. No normative PASS/FAIL for destroyed-owner behavior. An unobserved callback is not proof that its promise remains pending.

Six scenarios: loaded iframe and popup, each live, immediately removed/closed, and called 100 ms after removal/closure. Click once per scenario to provide a real user gesture for popups. Use a fresh, disposable profile.

Full matrix (seeded ready control) | Isolated register() (no seed worker). The full matrix uses an active worker to give ready a meaningful live control; the isolated mode checks whether that setup influences registration.
